Hypertrophy

- muscle fibers grow larger as a response to resistance or strength training
- how people gain strength and size through lifting weights or resistance exercises.
- someone consistently does bench presses, their chest muscles get bigger due to hypertrophy.

Slow-twitch fibers

- muscle fibers that contract slowly and are resistant to fatigue, making them ideal for endurance activities.
- important for long-lasting physical activities such as running or cycling where stamina is needed.
- marathon runner relies heavily on slow-twitch fibers to maintain energy and keep running over long distances.

Dynamic stretching

- type of stretching involves moving joints through a full range of motion in a controlled way.
- prepare the body for exercise by warming up muscles and preventing injury.
- high knees or arm circles before a basketball game is dynamic stretching.
